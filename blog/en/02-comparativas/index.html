The system is chosen by the roof, not by the brand. A silicone may perform on a sloped slab and fail on a flat slab that collects water; An asphalt membrane resists more, but adds weight and days of work. This table summarizes the starting point before comparing prices.

SystemWhere does it perform?Weak pointWhen to choose it
100% siliconeSloped slabs and roofsDoes not forgive trapped moistureDry surface and resolved cracks
PolyurethaneRoofs with traffic and jointsUV degrades it without finishingYou need elasticity and resistance
Elastomeric acrylicReflective roofs and direct sunLess bridging in active cracksYou are looking for reflectivity and simple maintenance
Asphalt membraneFlat slabs and multi-layer systemsAdd weight and days of workYou want a reinforced barrier
