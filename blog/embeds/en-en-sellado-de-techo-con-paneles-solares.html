What is each system

100% silicone

Pure, moisture-curing, single-component, solvent-free silicone. It is applied in one or two coats over primer. At joints and finishes it is reinforced with polyester mesh.

Coverage: Bull-Bond SIL HS is sold by the pail. Cintrón's King of Silicone claims 170 to 200 square feet per 5-gallon pail. Published duration: WindMar publishes 20 to 25 years. Danosa maintains that there is no independent study to support it, so that data is the maximum of the published range, not a guarantee.

Warranty: Bull-Bond offers an anti-leakage guarantee of 4, 5, 7 or 10 years, renewable if requested in time. Cintron promises extended warranty according to contract when they install.

Polyurethane

One-component or two-component polyurethane, with base, primer and finish. It is applied in three typical steps: primer, base and finish. Mesh is inserted in joints.

Coverage: Depends on the product. Cintron's Cintronator is sold by component, not by declared area. Published duration: WindMar publishes 10 to 15 years.

Guarantee: Depending on the system and the installer. Cintron stipulates it in the service contract.

Real advantages and disadvantages

Summary of what each system solves and what it does not.
Systemin favoragainst
Silicone 100%
  • Forms a single continuous layer, without seams or overlaps
  • Resists stagnant water better than any acrylic
  • Does not feed mold or fungi, because it does not have organic material
  • AHolds the thermal expansion and contraction of the slab very well
  • It does not breathe: if the slab is not dry, it traps moisture inside
  • It requires cleaning and drying that almost no one does completely
  • It cannot be applied on a wet roof or with rain nearby
  • It is the system with the highest price per square foot
Polyurethane
  • High resistance to traffic and friction
  • Good adhesion on concrete, metal and wood
  • Resists chemicals and UV rays with the correct finish
  • It is the natural system around equipment and anchors
  • Strong odor and slower curing than acrylic
  • Sensitive to moisture while curing
  • More expensive than acrylic
  • Surface preparation does not allow shortcuts

Which one is best for your roof?

Silicone 100% is justified when flat slabs, ceilings where water collects, joints, finishes and joints with walls.. Polyurethane, when ceilings with traffic, air equipment, terraces and technical areas..

If your roof hasstagnant water, open auctions or more than ten years without maintenance, neither of the two systems is chosen based on price: first the cause is corrected.

Agenda roof inspection

How to apply it on a roof in Puerto Rico

The order matters more than the brand. This is the process that the system requires, not the one that can be shortened to close the job in one day.

  1. Inspection and humidity testThe slab, the finishes and each penetration are checked. If there is trapped moisture, do not apply.
  2. Cleaning and dryingPressure washing, removal of loose material and wait until the substrate is dry.
  3. Correction of cracks and finishesThe cracks are opened, filled with polyurethane and the joints are reinforced.
  4. PrimerThe silicone-compatible primer is applied to ensure adhesion.
  5. Silicone membraneTwo hands crossed with the thickness calculated by area, not by eye.

The mistakes that ruin this work

  • Does not breathe: if the slab is not dry, it traps moisture inside
  • It requires cleaning and drying that almost no one does completely.
  • It cannot be applied on a wet roof or with rain nearby.
  • It is the system with the highest price per square foot
The most expensive mistake of all is applying to a surface that is not dry. If the forecast does not give a dry window, it is postponed: redoing costs more than waiting.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the price of roof sealing vary so much?

Because different things are being compared with the same name. A $1.75 per square foot job is typically a bucket coat of paint on a half-washed ceiling. A $6.50 system includes pressure wash, crack correction, primer, membrane and manufacturer's warranty. Ask what steps are included before comparing numbers.

Does roof sealing resist a hurricane?

Caulking is not a structural covering: it resists wind and rain if well adhered, but it does not support a roof that is already loose. Asphalt and thermoplastic membranes have wind certifications that can be verified on the product sheet. Ask for specific certification before believing a verbal promise.

Can you seal a roof that is leaking right now?

You can intervene, but it is not sealed over. First, the water ingress is stopped, the area is dried, and the cause is corrected: a crack, an open finish, a loose screw. Sealing over an active leak is ensuring it comes back. If there is water trapped in the slab, it must be let out before applying.

Questions and table on this page

Can a roof that has solar panels be sealed?

Yes, but you have to coordinate with whoever installed the panels and with the system warranty. Bull-Bond warns that drilling for panels or equipment before installing sealant may void your warranty unless its instructions are followed. The correct thing to do is to seal before installation, or disassemble and reassemble with the anchors sealed.

What system is best for sealing a roof that has solar panels?

The published system for that case is 100% silicone: slabs, flat roofs and exposed concrete. Its published duration is 20 to 25 years. Before applying it, the cause must be corrected—cracks, screws, overlaps, or slope—because no coating fixes a substrate that continues to move or accumulate water.
