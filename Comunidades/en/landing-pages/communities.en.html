      Communities FAQ

      What communities does it cover?Ilatek?

      IlatekIt has its own page for the 556 communities of {{custom_values.county_name_and_state}}, with 1,112 pages of roof sealing and cleaning in the 78 municipalities of Puerto Rico.

      How do I find my community on the map?

      Use the community map and filter by region—Metropolitan, North, East, South, West, or Central—or search for your community name. Each community has its page with services, weather and reviews.

      What services can I request in my community?

      Roof sealing and cleaning: waterproofing, repair, preventive maintenance, residential, commercial and deep cleaning, among others. Scope and price are confirmed in writing prior to service, with $50 visit or inspection deductible from the total.

      Do they serve my municipality even if my community does not appear?

      Yes. We cover the 78 municipalities of Puerto Rico; If your community does not appear on the map, schedule a visit or inspection: $50 deductible from the total service and we confirm coverage.

      How do I quote for my community?

      Pricing is calculated per job and confirmed in writing: cleaning is quoted from $250 depending on the size and condition of the space, and roofing work is quoted after assessing the area. You can start in cleaning quote o en el roofing estimator.
